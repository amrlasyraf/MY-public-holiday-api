# Official announcement source research

Checked: 26 September 2026. This is a research inventory. Subsequent implementation enabled Perlis plus Johor and Kedah RSS/archive monitoring alongside the two BKPP indexes; see [monitoring](monitoring.md). Other expansion entries remain research candidates. No holiday records or publication rules were changed.

## Findings

All 13 states and three federal territories have an entry below. Coverage is not complete merely because a government portal exists: an announcement may first appear in a speech, an image or an official social account. A working endpoint also does not establish that every declaration will be published there.

The strongest initial candidates are Perlis's press-statement PDF index and the Johor and Kedah RSS feeds. Penang also has a working RSS feed. Sabah and Sarawak have useful gazette indexes. Selangor's circulars are accessible through separate pages embedded in the portal, rather than through its sparse homepage.

Website ownership and declaring authority must be checked separately. A gazette or a statement from the relevant declaring office can support confirmation under [the source policy](source-policy.md); a government-hosted news story, newspaper clipping, municipal closure or personnel notice does not automatically qualify. Evaluate the actual document's date, issuer, geographic scope and affected population.

## Official examples for future evaluation

These are source-discovery examples, not new accepted records or a completed labelled evaluation dataset. Review original documents before assigning final labels.

- **Johor:** [SUK statement dated 9 December 2023](https://www.johor.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/20231209-SS-Kenyataan-Akhbar.pdf) explicitly names a special holiday on 17 December 2023. Useful for distinguishing statement date, celebration date and holiday date.
- **Kedah:** [2026 Thaipusam announcement](https://www.kedah.gov.my/index.php/2026/01/21/hari-tahipusam-sebagai-cuti-peristiwa-di-negeri-kedah-darul-aman-tahun-2026/) links supporting material. Useful for article-to-document retrieval and year checks.
- **Kelantan:** [2025 administrative archive](https://www.kelantan.gov.my/index.php/arahan-pentadbiran/57-2025) lists a special-holiday document for 30 March 2025. Useful for non-`.pdf` download routes.
- **Perlis:** [official media index](https://www.perlis.gov.my/index.php/suk-perlis/info-umum/media1/kenyataan-media) lists the 1 January 2025 special-holiday declaration published on 31 December 2024. Useful for short-notice detection across year boundaries.
- **Pahang:** [official communication denying an extra day in May 2024](https://www.pahang.gov.my/pahang/resources/PDF/Berita/2024/Mei/%282HB%29_TIADA_CUTI_TAMBAHAN_DI_PAHANG_UNTUK_CUTI_HARI_HOL_DAN_WESAK.pdf). Useful as a negative example: holiday keywords can describe a denial.
- **Sabah:** [27 March 2025 gazette](https://sabah.gov.my/gazette/docs/003148.pdf) includes a notification appointing 2 April 2025 as an additional holiday. Useful for locating a relevant notice within a larger gazette.
- **Sarawak:** [gazette announcement for Part II 2025, issue 52](https://sag.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/announcement_view/1361) leads to the additional-holiday instrument. Separately, [the 4 June 2025 notice](https://premierdept.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/18761/UKAS) expressly concerns Sarawak public services, statutory bodies and local authorities. These should not receive identical population-scope labels.
- **Cross-state corroboration:** [JTKSM press statements](https://jtksm.gov.my/en/media/press-statements) retrieved successfully and include holiday-related notices. [Its Negeri Sembilan notice](https://jtksm.mohr.gov.my/ms/kenyataan-media/kenyataan-media-cuti-peristiwa-negeri-sembilan-pada-3-ogos-2026) is a useful retrieval lead. Labour implementation guidance must remain separate from declaration evidence.

## Proposed rollout

1. **Perlis PDF index pilot (implemented).** Extracts table-row context and monitors holiday-related PDF candidates. Measure unrelated alerts and missed candidates before expanding. Other ordinary press statements are counted for index health but not downloaded.
2. **Johor and Kedah RSS discovery (implemented).** Reconcile the full public WordPress posts archive on every run, retain identities and article hashes, and fingerprint linked official PDFs. This catches posts outside the ten-entry feed window.
3. **Kelantan and Selangor document indexes; Penang RSS.** Add category/embedded-index traversal and route-based downloads. Verify that retained article/table text contains the date and subject needed for review.
4. **Sabah and Sarawak gazettes, then JWP and Pahang.** Add bounded pagination, year rollover and notice-level relevance filtering. Keep Sabah, Sarawak and Labuan applicability distinct. Many gazette issues concern unrelated matters.
5. **Terengganu and retrieval gaps.** Establish holiday-announcement coverage beyond the current news archive. Recheck Melaka, Negeri Sembilan and Perak from the GitHub runner; the local errors were `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY_LEAF_SIGNATURE`. Investigate the certificate chain/runtime trust store without disabling certificate validation.

For each pilot, inspect the site's current robots/publishing conditions and establish a modest polling rate before scheduling. This pass tested discovery endpoints; it did not approve automated access policies, assess all attachment sizes, prove feed completeness, or test every source on GitHub. An hourly index/feed check is a starting proposal; fetching full article/PDF content should be bounded and changes retained as evidence. Existing content checks remain necessary for documents revised at the same URL.

This document does not authorize automatic holiday publication or claim complete ad-hoc coverage.
